# Who the digest is written for

Edit this file freely. `riff digest run` pastes it into the prompt, so the more
specific it is, the more specific the ideas will be.

## What this is for

Staying curious, and solving problems that could not be solved before.

Riff is a bit of a dada machine: it pulls in raw material from unrelated
places, lets it collide, and through interaction and meandering something new
gets built that nobody set out to build. I want the digest to feed that, not
to manage my career.

## What I want from an idea

- A problem that was out of reach until recently, and the specific thing that
  now puts it in reach.
- Collisions: two sources that have no business being together, and what
  happens when they are forced to be.
- Things that are strange, playful, or slightly absurd but would actually run.
- A thread I can pull on and wander from. An open question is as welcome as a
  finished plan.
- Surprise. If I could have guessed the idea from the headlines, skip it.

## What I do not want

- Benchmarks, scorecards, and evaluation harnesses as the default answer.
- Ideas justified by how they would look on a resume.
- Improvements to Riff itself, unless they are genuinely odd.
- Generic "learn X" advice, news summaries, or hype without a mechanism.

## What I have to work with

- Python, Postgres, FastAPI, MCP, and model APIs.
- This system: a growing store of raw articles and notes that can be queried,
  recombined, and argued with.
